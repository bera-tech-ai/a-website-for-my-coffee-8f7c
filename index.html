Our Story

Founded in 2018, Bean & Leaf started as a small cart in the park. Today, we're a cozy neighborhood spot where friends meet, work happens, and every sip is savored. We roast our own beans and source pastries from local bakers.

We believe in slow coffee for a fast world.
